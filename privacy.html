Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

1. Who we are

GTA 6 Clipper ("the App") is a personal automation tool that publishes gaming videos to its owner's own TikTok account through the official TikTok API.

2. Data we collect

The App does not collect personal data from the public. When the account owner authorizes the App via TikTok Login Kit, TikTok provides:

  • an access token and a refresh token, used only to publish videos to that account;
  • basic profile information (user.info.basic: open ID, display name, avatar), used only to confirm which account is connected.

3. How data is used and stored

Tokens are stored encrypted as secrets in the App's private deployment environment and are used solely to upload videos through the TikTok Content Posting API. We do not sell, share or use this data for advertising or any other purpose.

4. Third parties

The App communicates only with TikTok (to publish videos) and Twitch (to retrieve public clip information through the official Twitch API).

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the App is authorized. Revoking access in the TikTok app (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions) immediately stops the App from posting; the stored tokens are then deleted.

6. Contact

For any privacy question or deletion request: github.com/roefwin2.
